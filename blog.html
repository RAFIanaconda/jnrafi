Understanding Markov Chain Deterioration Models for Pipeline Asset Management

In this post, I discuss how Markov chain models can be used to predict the deterioration of water pipelines over time. These probabilistic models form the backbone of modern asset management frameworks, allowing engineers to forecast condition states and optimize rehabilitation schedules based on life cycle cost analysis.
