Hi, I'm Julker Nayen Rafi

Graduate Researcher in Civil Engineering

Julker Nayen Rafi is a Graduate Research Assistant in Civil Engineering at Georgia Southern University, working under the supervision of Dr. Ram Krishna Mazumder. His research focuses on the risk, resilience, and asset management of civil infrastructure, with particular emphasis on aging water distribution systems and infrastructure exposed to natural hazards. His work integrates probabilistic risk analysis, infrastructure deterioration modeling, geospatial analysis, remote sensing, and machine learning to support more informed infrastructure management.

Prior to graduate research, Rafi gained extensive professional experience in structural design, safety assessment, inspection, and rehabilitation of buildings and industrial facilities.

Research Interests

Risk-Informed Asset Management

Developing risk-informed frameworks for rehabilitation planning and life cycle cost optimization of aging water distribution systems.

Risk Assessment under Natural Hazards

Evaluating infrastructure vulnerability under seismic, flood, and multi-hazard scenarios using probabilistic and GIS-based approaches.

Life-Cycle Cost Analysis

Applying Markov chain deterioration models and cost-benefit analysis for data-driven rehabilitation scheduling of water pipelines.

Leak Detection using Satellite Imagery

Developing computer vision models leveraging satellite imagery for automated leak detection in water distribution networks.

Trenchless Technology

Investigating trenchless rehabilitation methods for sustainable and cost-effective pipeline renewal strategies.

Structural Rehabilitation

Assessing structural safety and designing rehabilitation plans for aging infrastructure, drawing on 4.5 years of professional structural engineering experience.
